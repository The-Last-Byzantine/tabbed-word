EMAIL SETUP: "Email all" puts every open tab (as .docx) into one new draft with the files attached.
Do this once. Then send the two IDs to whoever rebuilds the app, or set them yourself (see the end).

============================================================
A) OUTLOOK.COM / MICROSOFT 365 (recommended, simplest)
============================================================
1. Open https://entra.microsoft.com and sign in with your Microsoft account.
2. Go to "Identity" > "Applications" > "App registrations" > "New registration".
3. Name: Tabbed Word
   Supported account types: "Accounts in any organizational directory and personal Microsoft accounts"
   Redirect URI: choose "Single-page application (SPA)" and enter
       https://tabbedword.pages.dev/
   (For testing on your own computer, also add http://localhost:8000/ under the same platform.)
4. Click "Register".
5. On the Overview page copy the "Application (client) ID". This is the microsoftClientId.
6. Go to "API permissions" > "Add a permission" > "Microsoft Graph" > "Delegated permissions",
   tick "Mail.ReadWrite", then "Add permissions". No admin consent is needed for personal accounts.

============================================================
B) GMAIL (Google Cloud)
============================================================
1. Open https://console.cloud.google.com and create a project named "Tabbed Word".
2. "APIs & Services" > "Library" > search "Gmail API" > "Enable".
3. "APIs & Services" > "OAuth consent screen":
   User type "External". Fill in the app name and your email addresses.
   "Scopes" > add  https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.compose
   "Test users" > add your Gmail address.
   Leave the publishing status on "Testing". Only the listed test users can sign in, which is fine for personal use.
4. "APIs & Services" > "Credentials" > "Create credentials" > "OAuth client ID":
   Application type "Web application".
   Authorized JavaScript origins:
       https://tabbedword.pages.dev
       http://localhost:8000   (only for testing on your computer)
   Click "Create" and copy the "Client ID". This is the googleClientId.
   Google may show a warning that the app is not verified. That is expected in Testing mode.

============================================================
WHICH ONE TO USE
============================================================
Outlook and Gmail both work. Pick one as the provider. Outlook is simpler to set up.

============================================================
SENDING THE IDs
============================================================
Send the provider ("outlook" or "gmail") and the ID(s). The app is rebuilt with them included.
The IDs are not secret, but do not share the app secret or any password. This setup does not need one.

Notes
- The first click asks you to sign in and allow access. Later clicks reuse that sign-in.
- Browsers may block the sign-in window. Allow pop-ups for the site if nothing opens.
- One draft can hold up to about 3.5 MB of files in Outlook, and about 20 MB in Gmail.
  Larger sets: use "Save all & close" and attach the files yourself.
- The drafts are not sent. You review and press Send yourself.
